THE STORY BEHIND THE BRAND

Making Everyday Discovery
Feel Simpler.

Everyday feeding routines are filled with little moments that matter. At 110 Baby Feeder, our aim is to create a welcoming place where parents and caregivers can explore feeding essentials, compare product details, and discover options that fit their everyday needs.

Feeding is not just a daily task—it is a quiet ritual of bonding, nourishment, and reassurance. We designed our collection to remove the noise, confusing medical jargon, and clutter, giving parents clear insight into bottle shapes, silicone softness, and easy-clean designs.
