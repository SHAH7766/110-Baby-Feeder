## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a premium, tranquil, and parent-friendly commerce experience. Rooted in mindful minimalism, it balances comforting warmth with clinical reliability. The interface evokes reassurance, ergonomics, and elevated nursery aesthetics—eliminating cognitive friction for busy, fatigued parents while celebrating the tactile beauty of baby feeding essentials.

### Style Archetype
- **Approach:** Minimal Warmth & Tactile Editorial Modern.
- **Atmosphere:** Clean, open spaces layered over warm milk-cream canvases, punctuated by calming coastal blues, herbal pastels, and soft pill-form geometry.
- **Emotional Intent:** Calm, safe, dependable, and delightfully intuitive.

## Colors

The palette avoids sterile hospital whites and harsh saturations, choosing instead gentle tones that echo natural nursery materials and ergonomic baby products.

- **Primary Canvas (`#FFF9F0`):** Warm milk-cream base providing natural warmth and softness across full-screen layouts.
- **Deep Marine Blue (`#326B91`):** The primary anchor. Communicates clinical trustworthiness, structural reliability, and clear visual authority on key conversion targets, active states, and focus rings.
- **Pastel Blue (`#A9D9F5`):** Expressive secondary hue for badge backgrounds, promotional tints, subtle callouts, and hover highlights.
- **Soft Peach (`#FFD9C7`):** Gentle accent tone representing warmth and tender care. Ideal for gentle alerts, product bundle tags, and organic certifications.
- **Sage Green (`#C9DCCB`):** Environmental and ingredient indicator (e.g., BPA-free, pure silicone, recycled packaging tags).
- **Charcoal Navy (`#243447`):** Primary text and icon shade offering high contrast without the jarring edge of pure black.
- **Clean White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-elevation floating cards, active tab switches, and sticky navigation headers.
- **Deep Night Navy (`#1B384E`):** Used exclusively for structural grounding in footers and checkout safety banners.

## Typography

The typography pairing uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural geometry, display elegance, and clarity, complemented by **Nunito Sans** for warm, readable, and fatigue-reducing body copy.

- **Eyebrow Typography:** Always set in uppercase with wide letterspacing (`0.12em`), providing a crisp architectural frame above hero headers and category titles.
- **Editorial Headings:** Display headers feature tight tracking and generous leading to maintain a contemporary lifestyle magazine aesthetic.
- **Product Pricing & Badges:** Rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold and bold weights to guarantee instant visual hierarchy at checkout, drawer carts, and collection feeds.
- **Body & Nutritional / Material Details:** Set in `Nunito Sans` for rapid legibility during late-night feeding sessions and small mobile viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

A balanced 12-column layout on desktop scales down to an 8-column layout on tablet and a single-to-two-column rhythm on mobile devices.

- **Canvas Rhythm:** Maximum layout width is capped at 1320px for centered readability, bordered by `3rem` (48px) gutters on desktop and `1.25rem` (20px) on mobile viewports.
- **Breathing Space:** Sections are separated by generous vertical padding (`space-xl`), allowing high-resolution product photography and textural details to stand out without cognitive clutter.
- **Micro-Gaps:** Buttons, chips, input fields, and pill selectors align consistently to the 8pt derived scale (`space-xs` to `space-md`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines warm ambient light with restrained glassmorphic layering, avoiding heavy dropped shadows.

- **Ambient Light Tints:** Drop shadows use tinted charcoal navy with high diffusion:
  - *Resting Card Elevation:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(36, 52, 71, 0.05)`
  - *Floating Interactive Elevation (Hover & Dropdown):* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(36, 52, 71, 0.08)`
  - *Modal & Drawer Elevation:* `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(36, 52, 71, 0.12)`
- **Low-Contrast Ghost Outlines:** Cards sitting on the warm cream background use a subtle outline of `1px solid rgba(50, 107, 145, 0.08)` to maintain boundary definition without visual noise.
- **Translucent Glass Accents:** Navigation headers, floating bottom checkout bars, and image badges employ a frosted surface: `rgba(255, 249, 240, 0.85)` with a `16px` backdrop blur.

## Shapes

The design adopts pill-shaped, organic geometry (roundedness level 3) across the entire component library. This tactile, baby-safe visual language eliminates harsh corners, mirroring the smooth curves of silicone nipples, ergonomic bottles, and rounded feeding utensils.

- **Buttons, Badges, and Inputs:** Full-pill radiuses (`9999px` or `rounded-xl` equivalent).
- **Product Cards and Feature Panels:** Large curved corners (`2rem` / 32px) on desktop, scaling to `1.5rem` (24px) on mobile.
- **Image Frames & Visual Dividers:** Elliptical curves and continuous smooth radius transitions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart / Buy Now):** Deep Blue (`#326B91`) solid fill, white text (`#FFFFFF`), full pill shape, height `52px`, horizontal padding `28px`. Hover shifts to darker tone with a subtle soft blue ambient glow (`0 8px 20px rgba(50, 107, 145, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#326B91`, text in `#326B91`. On hover, fills with a light wash of `#A9D9F5` at 20% opacity.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Pure Charcoal Navy text with an animated horizontal underline and soft 4px hit state expansion.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Fully rounded pills (`height: 36px`, `padding: 0 16px`).
- **Unselected:** Warm white surface (`#FFFFFF`), `1px` soft border in `rgba(50, 107, 145, 0.15)`, text in `#243447`.
- **Selected:** Deep Blue (`#326B91`) background with crisp white typography and a small checkmark icon.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Pill-shaped input fields (`height: 52px`) enclosed in a pure white background with a subtle border (`1.5px solid rgba(50, 107, 145, 0.15)`).
- **Focus State:** Border transitions smoothly to Deep Blue (`#326B91`) with a `0 0 0 4px rgba(169, 217, 245, 0.4)` halo.
- Labels sit above the field in `eyebrow` styling or `label-sm` weight for effortless scanning.

### Product & Review Cards
- White base (`#FFFFFF`) sitting over the `#FFF9F0` cream canvas.
- Curvature set to `rounded-lg` (32px).
- Product photography rests within a soft neutral inset pill frame.
- Badges (e.g., "Anti-Colic", "BPA Free", "100% Medical Grade") float in top-left corners using pastel fills (`#A9D9F5`, `#FFD9C7`, `#C9DCCB`) paired with Charcoal Navy micro-text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Radios are full circles; checkboxes are gently rounded squares (`border-radius: 8px`).
- Unchecked states use a soft `1.5px` outline in `rgba(36, 52, 71, 0.25)`.
- Checked states trigger an animated scale-in of `#326B91` with a clean white inner dot or checkmark.

### Quantity Selector (E-Commerce Essential)
- Pill container in white (`#FFFFFF`) with a subtle outline.
- Minus and plus icon buttons recessed within circular touch targets (`36px × 36px`), providing tactile tap feedback with zero layout shift.
